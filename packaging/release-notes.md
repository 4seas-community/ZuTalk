# ZuTalk 0.7.0

Your library on every Mac you own, a topic shared with a colleague,
transcripts handed across the room, and a backup on a Mac of your own —
all directly between Macs, with no account and no server keeping a copy.

ZuTalk requires macOS 12.5 or later.

## Your Macs, in sync

- **Turn on sync in Settings › Devices,** create a pairing code on one Mac
  and paste it on the other. Recordings, transcripts, corrections, notes,
  marks, topics and speaker names stay the same on every Mac, and edits
  made on two Macs merge.
- **Directly between your Macs.** On the same network they connect
  directly; elsewhere, ZuTalk's relay passes along encrypted traffic it
  can't read. No account, and nothing is stored on a server.
- **Audio stays on the Mac that recorded it.** Other Macs show where a
  recording was made — *From Studio* — and point you there to refine it.
- **A recording appears on your other Macs once it's finished,** not while
  it's still changing.
- **Remove a Mac** and it keeps what it already has, is told it was
  removed, and can start over. Any Mac can also leave the group.

## Work on a topic with someone else

- **Collaborate…** in a topic's menu creates an invite code for that topic.
  The other person pastes it under Topics › *Join shared topic* and the
  topic opens on their Mac.
- **Only that topic travels:** its recordings' transcripts, corrections,
  notes and marks. Your other topics, recordings outside it and all audio
  never do.
- **See who's in and who's online.** Whoever shared the topic can remove
  someone or stop sharing; everyone keeps what they already have.
  Deleting a shared topic leaves the collaboration first, so it never
  deletes anyone else's copy.

## Nearby, on the same network

- **Hand a transcript to a nearby Mac** from a recording's Share panel.
  It arrives only after the other person accepts, with its notes and marks,
  under *No topic* and marked as coming from you. To receive, turn on
  *Receive transcripts from nearby* in Settings › Devices.
- **Let ZuTalk on the same network watch your live captions** — a switch
  in the live panel, next to the link and independent of it. People nearby
  find it at the top of *New recording* and watch in a window of their own;
  nothing is saved on their Mac. It ends when your recording does.
- **Your Mac's name is visible nearby** only while receiving or sharing
  live nearby is on.

## Encrypted backup to another of your Macs

- **In Settings › Devices › Backup,** create a backup code and paste it on
  the Mac that will keep the backups. From then on it backs up every day,
  skipping days with no changes, and keeps the ten most recent — encrypted
  on disk.
- **Text only.** Audio is never backed up.
- **Restore to any Mac** with a restore code from the Mac keeping the
  backups, or straight into that Mac if your main one is gone. Restoring
  merges: nothing already there is overwritten, and anything permanently
  deleted stays deleted.

## Faster and steadier

- **ZuTalk opens faster.** Every launch used to redo all of the recovery
  work from earlier launches — over two seconds on a library of a hundred
  recordings. It now does only what's left.
- **A stopped recording no longer looks live,** and its transcript no
  longer gets stuck loading after you press Stop.
- **Less work while recording.** The main window redraws the live
  transcript at reading speed, and making a refined transcript no longer
  makes the app hitch every few seconds on long recordings. Opening a
  recording returns at once in a library with many topics.

The first time sync is turned on, macOS asks to let ZuTalk use the local
network — that is how Macs on the same network find each other.
