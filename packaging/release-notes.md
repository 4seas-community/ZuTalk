# ZuTalk 0.5.8

Settings now shows which version you are running.

ZuTalk requires macOS 12.5 or later.

- **The software update panel shows the installed version**, with its build
  number, and the text can be selected and copied. Previously there was no
  way to tell from inside the app what you had — awkward right after an
  update, and awkward when reporting a problem.
