# ZuTalk 0.6.1

With an invite, the passages you mark are tidied up for you — no language
model key of your own needed.

ZuTalk requires macOS 12.5 or later.

- **Invites now include tidying marked passages.** Turn on *Clean up marked
  passages* in Settings › General and a passage you mark comes back as
  readable sentences a few seconds later, without entering a key. Settings
  shows when your invite covers it and how many passages are left today
  (up to 300 a day).
- **Where the passage goes, said plainly.** On an invite, a marked passage
  goes through ZuTalk's invite service to DeepSeek to be tidied up; the
  service keeps nothing. Only passages you mark are sent — never audio,
  never the full transcript — and nothing is sent until you turn the switch
  on.
- **Your own key still comes first.** If you have saved an Anthropic key,
  passages go only to Anthropic and never through the invite service.
