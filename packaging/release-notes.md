# ZuTalk 0.7.2

Transcribe an online meeting straight from your Mac: ZuTalk can now record
what the Mac plays — the other side of a Zoom, Teams or Meet call, a video —
instead of the microphone.

ZuTalk requires macOS 12.5 or later. Recording system audio requires
macOS 14.2 or later.

## System audio as an input

- **Choose *System audio (meetings, videos)* under Audio input** in a
  recording's settings. ZuTalk then transcribes and translates what the Mac
  plays, so the people on the other end of a call are captured even when
  you wear headphones and the microphone never hears them.
- **Your own microphone is not included** while System audio is selected,
  and ZuTalk leaves its own sounds out of the recording.
- **Switch during a recording,** the same way you switch microphones.
- **The first time, macOS asks to allow system audio recording.** If a
  recording stays silent, turn ZuTalk on in System Settings › Privacy &
  Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.
- Audio recorded this way is treated like any other recording: encrypted,
  kept only on this Mac, and never shared.
