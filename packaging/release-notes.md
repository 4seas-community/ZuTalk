# ZuTalk 0.8.0

Residents of a city can now sign in and file a recording's transcript, in
every language it was captured in, into the city's archive under the right
event.

ZuTalk requires macOS 12.5 or later.

## Filing to a city's archive

- **Sign in once, as yourself.** Settings has a new section, City. Enter the
  city's address, then on a device where you're already signed in open
  City hall → Identity, choose Link a new device → Scan, and scan the code
  ZuTalk shows. Both devices show the same six digits before anything is
  handed over. You can also sign in with your recovery key. Signing in lets
  this Mac act as you in that city, so sign out when you're done on a Mac
  you share.
- **Choose the event before you press Record.** The Start Recording page
  has a new row, Event. When exactly one event is under way it is chosen
  for you; when there are several, or none, pick one from the city's
  calendar and the day's event list, or record without filing. Recording
  never waits for this choice.
- **The transcript files itself when you stop.** The original text and each
  translation go to the event's materials, aligned by time, along with
  speaker names and the places you marked if you leave those on. Stopping
  doesn't wait for the upload. Without a network the recording waits in a
  queue and is filed once you're back online.
- **A topic can have a channel.** In a topic's settings, choose an archive
  channel, and that topic's recordings are placed there as well.
- **See and change where a recording went.** The recording bar shows the
  event while you record, and lets you switch it or decide not to file. In
  a recording's Share panel, the new Archive section shows what was filed,
  sends corrections you made afterwards when you ask it to, and withdraws
  the item.
- **You can file only where the city lets you.** ZuTalk checks first that
  you host or record the event, or coordinate the city, and tells you who
  to ask if you don't. Nothing is sent that the city would not show.
- **Audio is never filed.** Only text leaves this Mac, as before.

What you file is public and stays in the city's records. Withdrawing takes
the item off the archive page, but text that was already filed cannot be
deleted from the city today. In the city's page the item has the transcript,
languages, speakers and marks, but no sound, so its play button does nothing.

Nothing changes if you don't sign in to a city.
