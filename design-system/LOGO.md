# Zulangue logo assets

The product name is **Zulangue**. The lettering inside the primary logo is
**ZuLangue**.

## Runtime variants

- `ZulangueAppIcon.png` is the canonical 1024 px full app icon. It keeps the
  complete `ZuLangue` lettering and is used for the 128–1024 px AppIcon slots.
- `ZulangueAppIconCompact.png` is the canonical 1024 px optical-small variant.
  It removes lettering and uses a heavier brush ring for the 16–64 px AppIcon
  slots.
- `ZulangueMark.imageset/zulangue-mark.svg` is the transparent monochrome
  template mark used by the 18 pt menu-bar item and the 24 pt sidebar brand.

Do not mechanically shrink the full wordmark into small icon slots. Keep the
AppIcon PNGs opaque and sRGB, and keep the interface mark transparent,
monochrome, template-rendered, and vector-preserved.
